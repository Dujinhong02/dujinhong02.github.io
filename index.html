ABOUT

Researching active robotic manipulation beyond single-step grasping.

My research targets dense, occluded, and geometrically incomplete scenes where direct grasping is unreliable. I study how robots can combine grasp generation, grasp evaluation, pose refinement, and non-prehensile actions to actively transform a difficult scene into a graspable one.

I have experience with simulation data generation, point-cloud learning, reinforcement learning, and zero-shot Sim2Real deployment on industrial robot platforms.

IEEE RA-L · Minor Revision 02

SuperGrasp

Single-View Object Grasping via Superquadric Similarity Matching, Evaluation, and Refinement

A single-view point-cloud grasping framework that transfers preliminary grasp poses through superquadric similarity matching, then evaluates and refines them with an anchor-guided E-RNet using gripper-closure geometry and local context.

SuperGrasp+ & PushPoint

Autonomous Robotic Picking for Large-Scale SKU Warehousing

SuperGrasp+ improves grasp reliability by jointly predicting gripper-width correction and collision risk. PushPoint models the target, nearby objects, and bin walls and uses SAC to learn goal-oriented non-prehensile actions that turn ungraspable states into graspable ones.
