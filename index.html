Jinhong Du 杜锦鸿

I am a Master's student in the School of Automation Science and Engineering at South China University of Technology (SCUT).

My research focuses on robotic manipulation in cluttered environments, including 3D point-cloud perception, grasp generation and evaluation, push-grasp synergy, reinforcement learning, and Sim2Real deployment.

I am particularly interested in enabling robots to move beyond single-step grasp prediction: when direct grasping is unreliable, the robot should actively rearrange the scene and create a better grasp opportunity.

GAPG: Geometry-Aware Push-Grasping Synergy for Goal-Oriented Manipulation in Clutter

Jinhong Du, et al.

IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation (ICRA), 2026 · Accepted

A geometry-aware push-grasp framework that jointly evaluates grasp feasibility and pushing benefit, enabling a robot to actively rearrange dense clutter before grasping a target object.

>90% grasp success · >95% task completion · zero-shot Sim2Real on ROKAE xMate ER7Pro.

SuperGrasp: Single-View Object Grasping via Superquadric Similarity Matching, Evaluation, and Refinement

Jinhong Du, et al.

IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters (RA-L) · Minor Revision

A single-view point-cloud grasping framework that transfers preliminary grasp poses using superquadric similarity matching, then evaluates and refines them with an anchor-guided E-RNet.

94.12% grasp success · 96.00% task success · 0.713 s inference time.

SuperGrasp+ & PushPoint: Autonomous Picking for Large-Scale SKU Warehousing

Project Owner · Industry Collaboration

Robotic Bin Picking · UR5e · 2026–Present

SuperGrasp+ predicts gripper-width correction and collision risk for robust grasping. PushPoint uses SAC to learn goal-oriented non-prehensile actions that turn difficult grasp states into graspable ones.

>80% grasp-only bin-clear success · >95% with PushPoint · tested on 50+ unseen items.
