Jinhong Du 杜锦鸿

I am a Master's student in the School of Automation Science and Engineering at South China University of Technology (SCUT).

My research focuses on robotic manipulation in cluttered environments, including 3D point-cloud perception, grasp generation and evaluation, push-grasp synergy, reinforcement learning, and Sim2Real deployment.

I am particularly interested in enabling robots to move beyond single-step grasp prediction: when direct grasping is unreliable, the robot should actively rearrange the scene and create a better grasp opportunity.

Email / GitHub / CV / Google Scholar

Research Interests

  • Robotic Grasping: 6D/7D grasp generation, evaluation, refinement, and collision-aware grasping.
  • Active Manipulation: push-grasp synergy and goal-oriented non-prehensile manipulation in clutter.
  • 3D Perception: point clouds, PointNet/PointNet++, GraspNet, and geometry-aware representations.
  • Robot Learning & Sim2Real: reinforcement learning, simulation data generation, and real-robot deployment.
